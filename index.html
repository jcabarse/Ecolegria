Driven by the Community.

At ECOLEGRIA, our mission is to upcycle local waste into high-quality, valuable products. By doing so, we are creating sustainable livelihood opportunities for the women of our community and building a cleaner, greener Ormoc City.

  • Manufacturing: Transforming discarded PET, HDPE, LDPE, glass, and paper into functional consumer goods.
  • Retail: Selling upcycled products directly back to local landscaping firms and neighboring LGUs.
  • Reinvestment: 100% of gross margins are reinvested into facility upkeep and our volunteer Eco-Points subsidy program.

High-durability, functional consumer goods crafted entirely from locally collected waste materials.
